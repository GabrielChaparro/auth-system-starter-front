FAQ

Is this a SaaS?

No. This is a self-hosted kit. You run everything on your own infrastructure. You keep full control over data and deployment.

Does it replace Keycloak?

No. It builds on top of Keycloak, providing a cleaner integration layer and a ready-to-use configuration so you don’t start from scratch.

Can I use it in commercial projects?

Yes. The commercial license allows you to use it in client work and internal company projects. Redistribution of the kit itself is not allowed.

Can I customize it?

Absolutely. You can adjust roles, realms, flows, UI, and extend the wrapper to fit your stack and requirements.
